# Writing Master Lists — Huge Desk Reference

A free desk reference for fiction: faces, gestures, emotions, appearance, sensory detail, settings, actions, and more.

**How to use:** Pick one or two cues that fit *this* character in *this* moment. Signature habits beat generic nodding. Mix inner sensation + visible behavior + one sensory detail from the room.

**Download tip:** Open `Writing-Master-Lists.html` in a browser → **Ctrl+P** → **Save as PDF**.

## 26. Dialogue Action Beats
- She set the mug down hard. “We’re done.”
- He didn’t answer until the door shut.
- She kept walking. “You coming?”
- He rubbed his face. “Say that again.”
- She handed him the keys. “Don’t make me regret this.”
- He laughed once, no humor. “Sure.”
- She looked at the exit, then at him. “Five minutes.”
- He checked the time. “You’re late to your own apology.”
- She spoke to the windshield. “I heard you.”
- He stood. Chair legs scraped. “Then prove it.”
- She whispered it like a secret and a threat.
- He said it to his hands, not to her.
- Silence stretched until someone had to break it.
- She answered a question he hadn’t asked yet.

Prefer beats + plain “said” over stacking exclaimed / retorted / snarled.

## 27. Plot / Conflict Sparks
- A secret costs more to keep than to confess — until confession destroys someone else.
- Two people want the same scarce thing for opposite moral reasons.
- A favor called in at the worst possible time.
- The safe place isn’t safe anymore.
- Someone returns who was supposed to stay gone.
- A plan works — and creates a worse problem.
- Loyalty to a person vs loyalty to a principle.
- The evidence is true; the story around it is a lie.
- A deadline that can’t move; a person who won’t.
- Help arrives from the last person they’d accept it from.
- A public win that feels like a private loss.
- The map is wrong / the witness is lying / the mentor is compromised.

## 28. Stuck Mid-Scene Prompts
1. What is this character trying *not* to show?
2. Where are their hands / feet / eyes?
3. What’s the strongest sense in the room right now?
4. What would make this conversation harder in the next line?
5. What object can do emotional work?
6. If they left the room now, what would that reveal?
7. What lie are they telling themselves in this beat?
8. What does the other character misunderstand?
9. Cut one sentence of explanation; replace with a gesture.
10. Add one concrete detail that only this character would notice.

## ADDITIONS — Action, Melee, Wounds, Happy Village

*(Also in the HTML sidebar: Running & movement / Melee weapons / Getting hit / Happy medieval village.)*

### Running & physical action
Boots hammering; breath tearing · stitch under the ribs · tasting iron and dust · skidding on wet cobble · pack/weapon slapping in rhythm · legs heavy while the mind insists on speed · glance back; pursuer gaining · vault a wall, land wrong, limp onward · footsteps sync then fall out of sync · fight stance: soft knees, chin tucked, circle for distance · feint draws a flinch · clinch too close for pretty technique

### Melee fighting (fiction-accurate notes)
- **Measure/distance** matters as much as the blade. Long weapons win at range; short weapons win if they close inside.
- Cuts often driven by hips/shoulders, not arm-flailing. Thrusts are often faster/harder to see than big swings.
- Armor may stop an edge and still leave bruises or broken bone underneath.

**Swords:** high guard threatens descending cut · point-forward threatens thrust · diagonal downward cut as workhorse · rising cut under shield line · thrust after parry · at close range: pommel, hilt, wrestling toward dagger · sword vs dagger: dagger tries to step offline, beat blade aside, get inside

**Daggers:** close work, thrusts, short cuts, grapples · target gaps not plate · narrow puncture often quieter-looking than a sword chop

**Polearms:** keep them at the tip · thrusts + butt strikes · edged heads cut with long leverage · if foe gets inside the shaft’s arc, shorten grip or drop to dagger/grapple

**Axes/chops:** cut + blunt shock together; slower recovery after a miss

**Blunt (mace/hammer):** dump force through padding; stun, numb limb, ring the skull without a neat wound

### Getting hit (by type)
**General:** blank half-second · then heat/wet/pressure · hand goes to the wound · breath out · delayed pain · keep moving on adrenaline

**Sword slash:** bright line of heat · wound gaps when muscles flex · heavy blows also bruise/shock bone

**Thrust (sword/spear):** sudden deep occupancy · hard to full-breathe if torso · may look “cleaner,” still devastating inside

**Dagger:** intimate distance · narrow puncture; cloth darkens before you understand · multiple short shocks

**Axe/chop:** thud in the teeth + cut · bruised wound edges · arm numb behind a shield hit

**Blunt:** bomb of force · numbness then throb · head: white flash, underwater sound · ribs: every breath sharp

**Lines:** The cut didn’t hurt until he tried to lift the shield. · The thrust felt like a fist that kept going. · Mail stopped the edge and let the bruise bloom underneath.

### Happy medieval-ish village
**Look:** packed lane to a green · whitewashed timber + tended thatch · wattle garden fences · small stone church · well as gossip hub · straight friendly smoke · geese as traffic · mill wheel flashing · strip fields beyond the last house

**Sound:** familiar bells · dawn whetstones · off-key work-song · anvil rhythm · ale-bench laughter · children’s feet on dirt · feast fiddle

**Smell:** bread + woodsmoke · hay · herbs/onions · beeswax · feast roast and ale · manure at the edges, not the center

**Feeling:** neighbors by name · roof-raising with cider after · a gate that still feels like welcome · strangers offered a stool before questions · kids who’ve never had to lock a door

**Lines:** The village looked like it had been loved longer than it had been built. · Smoke smelled like supper. · You could hear the mill and the blacksmith answering each other across the green.

## ADDITIONS — Internal Thoughts

### How thoughts work on the page
- **Direct thought** (often italics): *Don’t look at the door.*
- **Tagged:** She thought, Don’t look at the door.
- **Free indirect:** narration borrows their voice — Of course the door was locked. It always was when she needed it open.
- **Sense → meaning:** Heat climbed her neck. Everyone had heard. Perfect.
- **Under stress:** fragments. Blood. Whose. Move.
- Stay in one head per scene; let thought sound like that person; use it to show what dialogue hides.

### Examples
- *Smile. Just smile. You can fall apart after.*
- “I’m happy for you,” she said, and thought, *Liar. You’re rotting.*
- Of course he’d brought flowers. He always brought flowers when he’d done something unforgivable.
- The look on his face was the sweetest thing she’d ever seen, and that terrified her more than anger would have.
- The thought arrived fully dressed: *You’re going to ruin this.*

## ADDITIONS — Special Looks, Personality Lines, Clothing

### Special looks (accurate notes + lines)

**Piebaldism** (from birth, usually stable; classic white forelock; pale skin patches on forehead/trunk/mid-limbs; vitiligo tends to appear later):
- A white forelock cut a clean triangle into otherwise dark hair…
- Pale skin opened like a map across his chest — a true absence of color, edged darker where pigment crowded the border…
- One eyebrow half-ink, half-frost…

**Albinism / bone-white hair** (distinct from aged gray or dye):
- Hair the white of bone china — pale as if color had never been invited…
- Lashes so light they vanished until light caught them…
- Skin that burned under mild sun; hats as necessity…
- In candlelight soft gold; under fluorescents stark blue-white…

**Ginger / red shades:** strawberry blonde (peach-gold) · copper/ginger (penny-bright) · auburn (ember red-brown) · mahogany/wine · freckles like cinnamon · wet = darker/serious; dry = catches light

**Heterochromia:** one storm-gray, one hazel · sectoral amber wedge in blue · people don’t know which eye to trust · when angry, both look the same: hard

Also: vitiligo (acquired/spreading), birthmarks, premature gray streak, alternating black/silver nails, etc.

### Personality-rich lines
- The look on his face was the sweetest thing she’d ever seen.
- He smiled like he’d been saving it for her.
- His face shut like a shop at closing time.
- She smiled with her mouth and left her eyes out of it.
- He tried for charming and landed on earnest.
- She looked expensive and tired in equal measure.
- He entered the room like a correction to it.
- At first glance soft; at second glance armed.

### Clothing detail (incl. photo-inspired looks)

**Toolkit:** silhouette → texture → contrast → one accessory → character tell

**Gothic dandy:** poet blouse with cloud sleeves + stacked wrist ruffles · jabot frill + cameo · double-breasted black waistcoat with antique-gold buttons · high black trousers · white hair over black like spilled milk on ink · nails chessboard black/silver

**Forest knit fantasy:** asymmetrical forest-green cable knit · one shoulder bare on a leather strap · flower brooch + beaded cords · caramel belt cinching fringe flare · tan corduroy pleated skirt with matching braided fringe

**Monochrome utility:** second-skin black turtleneck · sleeves to elbows · oversized matte cargos · silver buckle · hands deep in pockets · narrow then suddenly wide silhouette

**Lines:** She dressed like a mood board that had won an argument. · He wore black the way some people wear silence. · Lace at the throat, knife at the belt.
